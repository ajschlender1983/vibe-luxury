The resonance call

B2C · HNW biohacker · 15 min · warm applicant
Open — earn the room
"Before I tell you anything about VIBE — what made you apply? What are you actually trying to change?"
Let them talk. You're qualifying for fit, not selling. The right founder wants to build, not just buy.
Discovery — find the gap
"Walk me through it. Where are you now — energy, sleep, focus — and where do you actually want to be? What have you tried that didn't get you there?"
This is the 80. Keep asking, stay quiet. The gap between those two answers is the whole sale; you're only naming it back to them later.
Read the buyer
Alpha → make VIBE feel like their discovery. Beta → give a clear, direct game plan. Owner → sell time back. Older → sell certainty and security.
Match the frame to the person before you say a word about the product.
Reframe — not another gadget
"You've probably tried a lot. Here's what's different: most of what you own manages symptoms. This works one level down — at the cell."
Proof — the measured claim
"In our testing, twenty minutes cut free-radical load by about a quarter, and a week of use raised the mitochondria's oxygen consumption. That's not relaxation. That's the thing generic sound beds don't do."
Say it plainly, then stop. Let the number sit.
Belonging — the circle
"The first ten are delivered. We're completing a founding fifty — the people who help shape what this becomes, and who I'll see in Mexico. It's a circle, not a checkout."
Close — the honest ask
"If this is a fit, I'd love you in the founding fifty. If it isn't, I'll tell you — no pressure either way. Where's your head?"
If they push back
"It's expensive.""It is. It's a hand-made, measured device and a founding seat, not a mass-market gadget. The question isn't the price — it's whether the outcome is real for you. That's what the founding trial is for."
"Isn't this just a vibration bed?""That's the exact thing we're not. Vibration is the category; the frequency is ours, and it's the reason the mitochondrial numbers move. I can send Dr. Steven's TED talk."
"Let me think about it.""Of course. Founding seats are limited and member-referred, so I can hold yours for a week. What would you need to see to feel sure?"
