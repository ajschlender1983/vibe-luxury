The Positioning

The peace, sleep, clarity and closeness you've been reaching for don't begin in your mind, your calendar, or trying harder. They begin one layer beneath all of it — in a body that finally feels safe. It all begins in the body.

VIBE reaches the body directly through vibroacoustic sound, so change happens where the feeling actually lives. The governing insight: you've been trying to fix in your mind a problem that lives in your body — that's why nothing has worked. Lead with relief, not aspiration.

The Platform — the Question

The brand's center of gravity is the founder's own question, and we keep it a question: "Can precisely engineered sound do the work we've asked medication to do?" It's proprietary to VIBE, it invites the mechanism-literate reader to lean in, and — kept open, never answered — it stays credible and compliant. Lead with the question, not with the category's tired keywords.

Retired: the "___ is a vibe" rotator. For longevity-first buyers (Attia/Huberman/Oura, functional-medicine practitioners) the wordplay read as wellness-lite and undersold the science. The equity is the name VIBE, not the pun. Signature line: "Engineered resonance. Measurable repair."

Voice

  • Feeling leads, proof follows — human outcome first; science gives permission.
  • Restraint is the luxury — short sentences, white space, say less than you could.
  • Speak to the body — "your chest loosens," not "cortisol decreases."
  • Every claim is true — the 40% is a study result, past-tense, attributed; never "cures."
  • The medication line stays a question — we may ask whether engineered sound can do what medication does, but we never answer it, never name a disease or a drug, and never tell anyone to change their medication. Every health-adjacent number is our own early, cellular-level lab finding ("preliminary, internal, not yet peer-reviewed"), not a proven benefit to the body.
  • Longevity is territory, not a promise — say "repair," "cellular vitality," "inspired by mitochondrial research"; avoid "cellular rejuvenation," "reverses aging," "extends lifespan." Route health-adjacent copy past counsel before publishing.
