## NEW BEAT · THE COHERENCE COLLECTIVE  (social proof through association)
*Placement: Act 5.5 — right after the proof stats, before the flagship. Or pair with Act 8's "one partner per market."*

### The strategic thinking (capture this — it's the engine of the beat)
The fastest way to answer a skeptic's *"is this legit?"* isn't more of our own claims — it's **borrowed credibility**. VIBE stands next to names the audience already trusts in the nervous-system / coherence / biohacking world (**HeartMath, BrainTap**, and others), under one banner: **The Coherence Collective.**

The mechanic that makes it nearly free to build:
- We invite those brands to become **affiliates** of ours — *"we'd love to sell and promote your work to our people."*
- For them that's an **easy yes** (free distribution, we do the selling), so they say yes.
- The moment they're in, we've earned the **right to show the association** — VIBE is now "in the same collective as HeartMath."
- The selection filter is aspirational: **"Whose brains and brands would we be proud to stand beside?"** Pick for the halo, not the transaction.

Net: a legitimacy layer that reframes VIBE from "another wellness gadget" to "a peer of the field's most trusted names" — and a genuine cross-promotion engine underneath it.
> Guardrail: only claim partnerships that are real and agreed. The affiliate relationship is the truth that earns the association — don't imply endorsement beyond what's signed.

### The beat, on screen (~10–14s)
- **VO:** "We're not doing this alone. VIBE is part of **The Coherence Collective** — a circle of the people and brands we most respect in the science of a settled nervous system."
- **Visual:** a slow, confident build of **partner marks** (HeartMath, BrainTap, + others) resolving around the VIBE diamond, on aubergine; not a busy logo salad — one at a time, breathing, premium.
- **VO:** "Different tools, one belief: regulation is the doorway. When you join VIBE, you join all of it."
- **SUPER:** *The Coherence Collective — partners in regulation.*
