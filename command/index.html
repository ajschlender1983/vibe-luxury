The trauma lens — PTSD & the mitochondria

The "nervous system on guard" the film opens with is, at its extreme, trauma. A growing research line ties chronic stress and PTSD to mitochondrial dysfunction, which is exactly where VIBE's measured effect sits. It's a large, underserved, emotionally urgent story, told honestly.

The scale

~13M

U.S. adults with PTSD in a given year (about 5%); roughly 6% will have it in their lifetime, and rates run far higher among veterans and first responders. Source: U.S. National Center for PTSD.

The link

Body-first

Trauma keeps the body in hyperarousal. Researchers (Picard, Mellon and others) increasingly associate PTSD and chronic stress with altered mitochondrial bioenergetics. VIBE works at that body level: nervous-system regulation plus the mitochondrial effect you measured.

Why it matters to the story

  • It completes the arc. Numbness → recognition → regulation is the trauma-recovery arc, told without hype.
  • Underserved and urgent. Veterans, first responders, trauma survivors — a group money and apps haven't solved.
  • Partnership surface. Veteran programs, trauma-informed clinics, nonprofits: mission-aligned distribution.

The guardrail (non-negotiable)

  • Not a medical claim. VIBE is wellness, not a PTSD treatment. Tell the story; never promise a cure.
  • Validate before you claim. The PTSD–mitochondria link is emerging research; cite it as such, and run the study before any clinical language.
  • Counsel first. Trauma and medical-adjacent language get legal review before anything goes public.

This is a story and a market, not a clinical claim. Keep it wellness-framed and honest until the research and counsel are in place.

Your move — the lead investor

You already have someone who wants to fund the whole round. Don’t let her own it — let her lead it. A circle of founder-investors beats a single check, on your cap table and in your funnel.

The play

  • Define the lead check. Set the qualifying lead position at $__ lead check size — the most you’ll take from any single investor. She fills it. You want a circle, not an owner.
  • Invite her to lead. “Take the lead check, be the named lead. The rest is for the founding 100.” People want to lead; few want to be one of many.
  • Reward going first. Her whole lead check comes in on a SAFE at a __% discount discount % — the full qualifying amount, not a slice of it. That’s her premium for leading.
  • Set the clock. The lead offer expires __ expiry date. After the date — or above the lead position — capital comes in at standard terms.

Why it works

  • More founders, not one owner. ~6 evangelist-investors on a $250K min beats a single buyer — each becomes a user and a referrer.
  • She still wins. Lead status, the discount, and the first customized VIBE.
  • A SAFE dodges the valuation fight. No price to argue now; you scale first and price the priced round later, higher.
  • The date does the closing. An expiring discount is honest urgency — not “one spot left.”

Run the exact SAFE terms (lead check size, discount %, close date) past counsel before you offer them. This is your negotiating strategy, not legal advice.
